Resume Builder

Build a professional resume in minutes — free, private and no sign-up

This is a free online resume builder that runs entirely in your browser. Type your details on the left, watch the live preview update on the right, and export a clean, print-ready A4 resume as a PDF. Choose from 20 professional templates — classic, minimal, modern sidebars, timelines, photo layouts and more — pick an accent colour and font that suit the role, add a profile photo, and download in one click. Your data is never uploaded to a server, so your personal information stays on your own device.

How it works

  1. Fill in your details — name, contact, summary, experience, education, projects, skills and certifications.
  2. Make it yours — choose from 20 templates, upload a photo, and pick an accent colour and font.
  3. Download as PDF — click “Save as PDF”, choose “Save as PDF” as the destination, and you are done.

Why people use it

  • 20 professionally designed templates to match any role.
  • Live preview, so there are no surprises in the final PDF.
  • Optional profile photo — resized on your device.
  • No account, no watermark, no hidden charges.
  • Fully responsive: works on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops.

Made for real applications

The output is a standard A4 sheet, which is what recruiters, ATS systems and job portals expect. Keep it to one or two pages, lead each bullet with an action verb, and quantify results wherever you can. Add only the sections you actually need — an empty section never appears in the resume.

Frequently asked questions

Is this resume builder really free?

Yes. This resume builder is completely free to use. There is no sign-up, no subscription and no watermark on the resume you download.

How many resume templates are there?

There are 20 professionally designed templates, ranging from classic and minimal to modern sidebars, timelines, photo layouts and more. You can switch templates at any time and the preview updates instantly.

Can I add my photo to the resume?

Yes. Upload a profile photo in the Personal info section and it appears in every template that has a photo area. The image is resized in your browser and never uploaded to a server.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed. Everything runs inside your browser and your entries are saved automatically on your own device.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Click “Save as PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” as the print destination. You get a clean, print-ready A4 resume that you can attach to any job application.

Is my personal data private?

Yes. The builder runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent to a server, so your personal details never leave your device.

Does it work on a mobile phone?

Yes. The layout is fully responsive and adapts to phones, tablets, laptops and desktop screens. On small screens you can switch between the editor and the preview with the Edit / Preview buttons at the top.
